About Me

Georgios Michail Makrakis (a.k.a George Makrakis) main interests are Cybersecurity and Software Engineering especially on Industrial Internet of Thins (IIoT). Currently works as OT Security Consultant at Hudson Cybertec \ Kiwa NL. Holds commercial experience as Cybersecurity Researcher and Software Engineer. Has proven ability to prioritize tasks, collaborate with external parties, and design robust solutions. Graduated from the University of the Aegean with an Integrated Master’s in Information & Communication Systems Engineering specializing in cybersecurity and software engineering, and owns a Doctorate (Ph.D.) in Computer Science from University of Idaho.
